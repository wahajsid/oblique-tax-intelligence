Methodology

How we calculate, and how sure we are

Every figure comes from a versioned rule with a date, a source and a review status. Same inputs, same answer, same trace, every time.

Review statuses

VerifiedChecked against the legal text. Where the law needs interpreting, we state Oblique's reading, which is in line with the prevailing view among practitioners.
Verified (secondary)Consistent across the main practitioner sources; the legal text has not yet been checked line by line.
Pending verificationReported but not yet confirmed, or sources disagree. Provisional.
Draft lawAnnounced or in draft. Not in force.

Simplifications you should know about

  • UAE Corporate Tax: single 12-month period, no Tax Group, no transitional relief elections, participation exemption conditions assumed met where exempt income is entered, DMTT top-up estimated with GloBE income set equal to taxable income.
  • KSA Zakat: year-end closing balances, no proration; the Article 28 upper-limit base rule is not applied because its formula was not confirmed from the regulation text.
  • VAT: domestic place of supply; input tax recovery ratio uses a simple taxable-to-total supplies proportion.
  • GCC comparison: AED thresholds (the UAE 375,000 band) are converted into the scenario currency at the indicative FX table (USD and SAR pegs are exact); KSA zakat uses profit as a proxy base; treaty rates ignored.
  • Move to the UAE: full-year, single, employment-income origin tax; split-year, exit taxes and retained home-source income are not computed; FX is indicative; no residence determination is made. US persons: residual US tax after FEIE with the stacking rule, FICA assumed to stop.
  • Scope gate: until you confirm scope and that no unmodelled regime applies, results are marked REVIEW_REQUIRED and shown greyed out.

Governance

Rules live in version control. Any change to a rate, threshold or date bumps the ruleset version and names the reviewer. Every result records the version it used, so it can be reproduced. AI never produces a rate or a liability here; it only explains.
