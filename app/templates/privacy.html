Privacy and data

Useful results, less data. This site is built so you can get a full answer without handing over anything about yourself.

  • Calculator inputs are sent to the calculation service only to produce your result and are returned to you in the response. They are not written to a database, not logged by the application and not used to train AI.
  • No cookies, no browser storage, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts and no third-party fonts (fonts are served from this site).
  • "Copy link" puts your inputs in the page address so you can share or revisit a scenario. That link lives in your clipboard and browser history only.
  • "Download result" creates a file on your device only when you click it.
  • Links to sources and to obliqueconsult.com open other websites with their own privacy practices.
  • Hosting infrastructure may keep ordinary technical access logs (IP address, time, URL) outside this application's control.

Enquiries and PDF working papers

The on-screen result is always free and anonymous. If you ask for the PDF working paper, a short form asks for your name, work email and, optionally, company, phone and a message, together with two separate consents:

  • Contact consent (required): Oblique Consult may contact you about the enquiry and process the details and the calculation you submitted for that purpose.
  • Marketing consent (optional, unticked): occasional GCC tax updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Controller: Oblique Consult, Dubai and Sharjah, United Arab Emirates (info@obliqueconsult.com). Lawful basis: your consent, and Oblique's legitimate interest in responding to a request you made. What is recorded: the fields you typed, the calculator name and the inputs you entered (so the adviser can see the context), the consent text version and timestamp, and a keyed hash of your IP address for abuse prevention (the address itself is not stored). Retention: 24 months from last contact unless an engagement follows. Your rights: access, correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent at any time by emailing info@obliqueconsult.com.

Where it goes: to Oblique Consult's mailbox and, where configured, to the firm's CRM or an automation service acting as processor on Oblique's behalf. The specific processors in use are listed in the form's privacy line. If no destination is configured on a deployment, the form is not shown and nothing is collected.

The PDF itself is generated on the server by recomputing your inputs and is sent to your browser; it is not stored.

Security headers

Every response carries a Content Security Policy, frame denial, no-referrer, nosniff and permissions-policy headers; API responses are marked no-store; browser requests from other origins are rejected; validation errors never echo what you typed.
